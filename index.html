Past Participle Flashcards

Practice English verbs. Remember the past participle.

What is a past participle?

Every English verb has a past participle form. We use it with have, has, and had.

Example: I have eaten breakfast.

Base verbSimple pastPast participle
walkwalkedwalked
gowentgone
writewrotewritten

Regular verbs add -ed. Irregular verbs change. You need to remember them!

How to play

  1. You see a card with a base verb, like go.
  2. Think of the past participle.
  3. Flip the card to check your answer.
  4. Mark yourself: I knew it or I need practice.
  5. Cards you miss come back again, so you can learn them.
